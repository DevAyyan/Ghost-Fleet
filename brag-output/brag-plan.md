# Brag Plan: Ghost Fleet X

## What is this app?
Ghost Fleet X is an open-source maritime intelligence platform that tracks dark vessels, coordinated unmonitored fleet maneuvers, and illegal fishing inside Marine Protected Areas using public satellite radar (Sentinel-1 SAR), nightlight radiance (NASA VIIRS), and spatio-temporal graph analytics.

## The angle
Ships deliberately turn off their AIS transponders to vanish from public view. Ghost Fleet X doesn't need AIS pings: it vectors their entry and exit trajectories, measures their physical radar oil slicks from space, calculates the exact co-presence window down to the minute when multiple ships operate dark together, and subjects every lead to a 7-variant Disproof Lab.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A high-contrast deep ocean bathymetry map (`#0c1017`) comes to life with glowing maritime coordinates. Text punches in: **"WHAT HAPPENS WHEN SHIPS GO DARK?"** An AIS transponder icon blinks, then cuts to static red: `SIGNAL TERMINATED: 23°14'N 67°05'E`.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Coordinated Dark Operations**: Two vessel vectors converge into an unmonitored zone. An amber halo locks around the encounter area: `⚡ 4 SHIPS · 38 HOURS DARK TOGETHER`. Exact UTC timestamps reveal the concurrent operation window.
- **Spaceborne Radar Verification**: Sentinel-1 SAR imagery exposes a 42 km radar oil slick attributed directly to a dark vessel wake where no AIS broadcast was recorded.
- **Epistemic Framework**: Non-accusatory case dossier with `OBSERVATION`, `INFERENCE`, `HYPOTHESIS`, and `RECOMMENDATION` leads.
- **The Disproof Lab**: The "Try to Disprove" engine tests the alert against 7 rigorous alternative analyses (Seasonal MK, trim start/end, strict coverage, coarser cells, neighbor control, strict $\alpha$). Result slams in: `✓ 7 OF 7 ALTERNATIVE ANALYSES SURVIVED`.

## Outro / punchline
The title slams in: **GHOST FLEET X**.
Subtitle: *"Discover maritime change. Test the evidence. Decide what to do next."*
